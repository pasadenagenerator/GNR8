--------------------------------------------------
1. BILLING PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------

GNR8 is not a SaaS tool.
GNR8 is an agency operating platform.

Billing must:

• enable frictionless agency adoption
• scale from 1 → 10 → 200 → 10,000 sites
• align platform incentives with agency success
• monetize value creation, not access
• support future marketplace economics
• support AI-native usage-based pricing
• support overrideable pricing strategies

Core principle:

Revenue must be derived from:
→ activity
→ intelligence
→ infrastructure value
→ orchestration leverage

NOT from:
→ login access
→ seat pricing
→ artificial feature gating

--------------------------------------------------
2. BILLING OWNERSHIP MODEL
--------------------------------------------------

Hierarchy:

GNR8 Platform
  → Agency
      → Client
          → Site
              → Runtime Artifact
                  → AI Activity

Key rule:

Published live sites are always client-owned.
Templates / drafts are agency-owned.
Experimental environments are agency-owned until publication.

Billing authority:

Agency is always the primary financial entity.
Client is always the operational beneficiary.

Early architecture assumption:

Client pays → funds flow to agency.
GNR8 extracts platform value via infrastructure + intelligence margin.

Future-ready design must allow:

• direct client billing (optional future)
• agency wallet systems
• multi-agency ecosystems
• marketplace settlement
