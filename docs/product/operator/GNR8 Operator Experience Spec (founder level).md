1. Purpose

GNR8 Operator Experience definira:

kako agencija dejansko uporablja GNR8 vsak dan.

Če tega ni:
	•	AI runtime je neuporaben
	•	migration factory je nevaren
	•	platform je samo infra

Operator Experience je:

👉 most med sistemom in business realnostjo

⸻

2. Primary User Archetype

Primarni user V1:

👉 Web agency operator

Ne:
	•	end-client
	•	freelancer designer
	•	random marketer

Agency operator:
	•	vodi več clientov
	•	dela migracije
	•	odloča o spremembah
	•	nadzoruje AI

⸻

3. Core Operator Mindset

Operator ne želi:
	•	“igrati builderja”
	•	premikati pixelov
	•	eksperimentirati brez nadzora

Operator želi:

👉 kontrolirano evolucijo strani

⸻

4. Main Operator Loop

To je glavni dnevni loop:

See site status
 → Review AI insights
 → Approve / reject proposals
 → Trigger migrations or changes
 → Monitor impact
 → Move to next client

GNR8 mora optimizirati:

👉 throughput operatorja

Ne:

👉 depth editing experience

⸻

5. Migration Experience

Migration mora biti:
	•	operacijski proces
	•	ne kreativni projekt

Operator vidi:
	•	import status
	•	validation issues
	•	preview
	•	approval gate
	•	publish readiness

To je:

👉 migration control tower

⸻

6. Post-Migration Experience

Po migraciji:

Operator ne dela redesign-a.

Operator:

👉 pregleda AI analizo

Sistem mora dati:
	•	site health score
	•	risk warnings
	•	opportunity list
	•	quick wins

⸻

7. AI Proposal Review Experience

Proposal review mora biti:
	•	batch oriented
	•	impact-oriented
	•	reasoning-transparent

Operator mora videti:
	•	kaj se spremeni
	•	zakaj
	•	kakšen vpliv ima

Ne:
	•	raw diff chaos
	•	visual noise

⸻

8. Execution Experience

Execution mora biti:
	•	atomic
	•	reversible
	•	trackable

Operator klikne:

👉 approve

Sistem naredi:
	•	novo verzijo
	•	deployment
	•	tracking

⸻

9. Multi-Client Context Switching

Agency mora:
	•	instantno switchat med klienti
	•	videti overview portfelja
	•	prioritetizirati delo

To pomeni:

👉 portfolio intelligence layer

⸻

10. Risk Perception Model

Operator mora vedno vedeti:
	•	kaj je safe
	•	kaj je risky
	•	kaj je strategic

UI mora komunicirati:

👉 risk semantics

⸻

11. Trust Building UX

Trust nastane ko:
	•	AI razlaga
	•	nič ne izgine
	•	nič se ne spremeni brez approvala
	•	rollback vedno deluje

Če trust ni:

👉 adoption fail

⸻

12. Notification Philosophy

GNR8 ne sme:
	•	spam-at operatorja
	•	ustvarjati alert fatigue

GNR8 mora:

👉 signal > noise

⸻

13. Autopilot Readiness Model

Operator mora:
	•	postopno zaupati AI
	•	najprej approve
	•	kasneje delegirati

Autopilot se uvede:

👉 evolucijsko

⸻

14. Operator Metrics

Sistem mora meriti:
	•	koliko strani operator upravlja
	•	koliko AI sprememb approve-a
	•	koliko impact-a ustvari

To je:

👉 agency performance intelligence

⸻

15. Approval as Core Primitive

Approval ni feature.

Approval je:

👉 centralni interaction pattern

Vse pomembno gre skozi:
	•	migration
	•	AI change
	•	publish
	•	billing changes
	•	destructive ops

⸻

16. Mental Model Simplification

Operator mora čutiti:

“GNR8 vodi spletne strani, jaz vodim GNR8.”

Ne:

“jaz ročno upravljam vsako stran”

⸻

17. Operator Failure Modes

Največji failure:
	•	preveč kompleksnosti
	•	premalo nadzora
	•	preveč AI magije
	•	preveč builder logike

⸻

18. Relationship to Chat Interface

Chat je:

👉 primary control surface

Ne:

👉 edini surface

Chat omogoča:
	•	ask AI
	•	trigger actions
	•	understand system

⸻

19. Long-Term Evolution

Operator experience evoluira v:

👉 agency cockpit

Kjer operator:
	•	vodi portfelj
	•	vodi AI
	•	vodi business growth

⸻

20. Founder Directive

Če operator experience ni:

👉 insanely clear

Potem:
	•	AI ne bo uporabljen
	•	migration ne bo skaliral
	•	product ne bo adopted

Operator UX je:

🔥 real adoption moat
