# Assumptions

These assumptions are implementation-only and do not reinterpret business truth.

- A dependency-free static source bundle is acceptable as a complete website
  source proposal for later import/review.
- English structural copy is acceptable for a proposal artifact, while observed
  source labels `O nas` and `Kontakt` are preserved exactly.
- The absence of verified contact details is best represented by a visible
  Kontakt placeholder rather than an invented phone, email, address, or form
  target.
- The absence of verified offerings is best represented by an explicit missing
  knowledge section rather than a service list.
- The absence of verified audience knowledge is best represented by explicit
  uncertainty rather than persona or customer copy.
- Imported assets are not embedded because the export states that asset signals
  may carry brand meaning but does not confirm logo or brand semantics.
